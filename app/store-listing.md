## App content questionnaires

**Ads**: No, the app does not contain ads.

**Data safety**
- Collects: *User IDs* (the username) and *Other in-app activity / game progress*.
- Shared with third parties: none.
- Encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS).
- Users can request deletion: yes, in the app (Settings → Delete Account); a web address for this is also required: use the privacy page above.
- No location, contacts, photos, files, device IDs or advertising IDs are collected.

**Content rating (IARC questionnaire)**: cartoon sports game. No violence, no blood, no sexual content, no profanity, no gambling (crates are earned with in-game coins and never give duplicates; their odds are shown), no drugs. Users can interact (online games, with preset quick-chat phrases only; usernames are visible and are filtered for rude words). The app does not share location. In-app purchases: none.

**Target audience**: the account holder decides. If children under 13 are included, Play's Families policy applies.

**Contains random items**: crates give random items, which Google requires the listing to say: the full description above mentions the odds.
